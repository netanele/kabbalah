# Synthesis brief: the cross-unit sections

**Purpose.** You write (new mode) or merge into (existing mode) the sections that tie the unit sections together: `summary`, `before`, `series`, `techniques`, `principles`, `books`. The unit sections already exist as `build/parts/NN-<section_prefix>-<n>.html`. The work is split across two agents: **agent A** owns `summary`, `before`, `series`, `techniques`; **agent B** owns `principles`, `books`. Never touch the other agent's parts, the unit parts or the skeleton.

**Inputs** (relative to `<workspace>`):
- `project.json`: `mode`, `unit_label`, `section_prefix`, `units` (which are `new`), titles, dates, durations.
- `<skill>/references/markup-guide.md`: the contract. Read §1 and §2 (files and rules), §5.7 (tables), §6 (status tags), §7 (content rules) and §8, which has the markup of every section you write.
- `build/parts/unit-<n>.manifest.md` for each NEW unit: (א) the technique cards with id, name, type, why, source; (ב) the books and sages; (ג) the key principles. Old units on an existing page have no manifests, because only the finished HTML is kept between runs. Their content lives in the exploded parts, so read those.
- `notes/<id>.md` of the new units: section 4 (principles), section 5 (books), section 9 (what was withheld), for depth.
- `build/parts/NN-<section_prefix>-<n>.html`: the real ids and titles. Link only to ids that exist (`grep -o 'id="[^"]*"'`).
- `meta/descriptions.md`: source titles, dates, durations.
- In **existing** mode: the existing synthesis parts, produced by `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/explode_page.py --config <workspace>/project.json` into `build/parts/10-summary.html`, `12-before.html`, `13-series.html`, `14-techniques.html`, `30-principles.html`, `31-books.html` (and the others), plus `build/skeleton.html` holding the live hero and footer.
- `research/background.md`: outside background. Not a source for these sections; the only exception is one `.note` if the contract allows it.

**Outputs.** The part files named above, each ONE `<section id="…">…</section>`, plus small generator scripts in `build/tpl/synth_*.py` for anything tabular.

## Rules shared with every fragment
Hebrew only. The four-letter Name never in full (הוי"ה, ה'). Other nations and religions generically. No inline style, script or img; `&amp;` in URLs. ids unique; the prefixes for ids you add are `sum-`, `bf-`, `sr-`, `ix-`, `pr-`, `bk-`. Document what was taught; do not recommend. Every claim about what the speaker said links to where it is on the page (`#t<n>-k`, `#<section_prefix>-<n>`) or to the source at that second (a `.src` chip, written out in full since these parts are not macro templates; copy the chip form from an existing part). No mention anywhere of how the page was made (no AI, model or tool names; the transcript credit is already in the transcripts section). Counts are worded "N כרטיסים", never as a claim that every card has steps, because reference cards and "mentioned only" cards have none.

## How to write: in small pieces
Agents in this pipeline lost hours by composing whole files in one generation. Create each part with its opening `<section>`, `h2` and `p.lead`, then append one card or one table chunk per write, each under about 1,500 words; the last append adds `</section>`. Tables built from the manifests are GENERATED by a small script you write (`build/tpl/synth_<name>.py`, run with `python3 -I`, reading the manifests and writing the rows); hand-write only prose. After each part, run `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/assemble.py --config <workspace>/project.json` and fix every report line about your parts.

## New mode: what each section holds
- **summary** (`10-summary.html`): 1–3 cards. `sum-series`: what the series or book is, how it is built, with chips for the speaker's own framing. `sum-units` (an existing page may call it something else, e.g. `sum-lessons`; keep its id): one `<li>` per unit, `<b><a href="#<section_prefix>-N"><unit_label> N</a> – topic.</b> one sentence.` `sum-page`: what the page offers (the card count, the per-unit blocks, the cross sections, the transcripts), closing with the `.note` that the page documents and does not recommend.
- **before** (`12-before.html`): the warnings and conditions the speaker himself sets, gathered from ALL units: who may practice, prerequisites, dangers, what is forbidden, what needs a teacher. Cards `bf-who`, `bf-danger`, `bf-forbidden` or similar, `ul.plain`, every bullet with chips or card links. Nothing here that was not said in the units.
- **series** (`13-series.html`): a `.lgrid` of `article.card.ls`, one per unit, id `sr-N`: `.ln` label, `h3` topic, `.d` with `<bdi>duration</bdi> · <bdi>date</bdi> · K כרטיסים`, one sentence, a `ul.inl` of the unit's 3–5 main cards, `.meta` with the `a.yt` (video) or source link, `a.go` to `#<section_prefix>-N`. Generate it from `project.json` plus the cards in each unit's part (count `article.card.tech`, pick the main cards).
- **techniques** (`14-techniques.html`): ALWAYS generated by `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/build_index.py --config <workspace>/project.json`, which SCANS every `article.card.tech` in all parts. It needs no manifests, and writes the section with the `ix-counts` line and the `table.idx`. Do not hand-write rows. If a row looks wrong, fix the card's h3, type tag or `מטרה` field, then re-run.
- **principles** (`30-principles.html`): what the speaker repeats across the series, grouped by TOPIC, not by unit: cards `pr-what`, `pr-who`, `pr-names`, `pr-amulets`, `pr-lots`, `pr-times`, `pr-purity`, `pr-halacha`, `pr-books`, `pr-learning`, as the material dictates. Each bullet close to the speaker's words, followed by one chip per place it was said. A principle that recurs in several units appears ONCE with all its chips. The `p.lead` says that who-may and what-is-forbidden are gathered in `before`.
- **books** (`31-books.html`): the merged list of every book, manuscript, source and sage from all units' manifests (ב) and notes section 5, two tables in `.tw`: `bk-books` (books, manuscripts, halachic and talmudic sources, alphabetical) and `bk-sages` (people), columns `שם | מי / מה | בשיעורים`, the last holding `<a href="#<section_prefix>-N"><span class="tag d">N</span></a>` per unit. The `p.lead` links to every unit's own books card `#l<n>-books`. A `.note` says names are spelled as heard, with `[?]` kept. Alias merging (same book under two spellings, a sage and his book) is done BY HAND after the script's first pass: keep the spelling most units use and list the other in the "מי / מה" cell.

## Existing mode: MERGE, never rebuild
The old content must survive untouched except where a count or a list grows. Work on the exploded parts in place.
- **summary**: add one `<li>` per new unit to the units list, in order; extend `sum-series` where the new units change the shape of the series (a new layer, a new year); update every count and total.
- **before**: add bullets or chips for warnings the new units add; attach a new unit's chip to an existing bullet when it repeats an old warning.
- **series**: add one `article.card.ls` per new unit; update the `p.lead` count.
- **techniques**: re-run `build_index.py`. It rescans every card in every part, old and new, so the whole index is rebuilt correctly without manifests.
- **principles**: for each principle in the new units' manifests (ג) and notes section 4, find the existing bullet that says the same thing and APPEND the new unit's chip to it; add a new bullet only for a principle the page did not have; add a new card only for a new topic.
- **books**: add rows for new books and sages; for a book the page already lists, add the new unit's `tag d` link to its "בשיעורים" cell and merge aliases by hand; extend the `p.lead` chain of `#l<n>-books` links.
- **Unit totals, everywhere.** The page states the unit count in words and digits in many places: the skeleton's hero `<p>`, `.dates` and footer, `summary`, `before`, `series`, `books` leads, the transcripts lead, the `.meta` tag on the index card. Find them all and update them:
  ```bash
  grep -n 'שמונת\|שמונה \|תשעת\|תשעה \|עשרת\|עשרה \|אחד־עשר\|שנים־עשר\|שלושה־עשר\|[0-9]\+ שיעורים\|[0-9]\+ כרטיסים\|כ־[0-9]\+ שעות' <workspace>/build/skeleton.html <workspace>/build/parts/1*.html <workspace>/build/parts/3*.html <workspace>/build/parts/90-*.html
  ```
  In this step agent A owns every count-bearing string in `build/skeleton.html`:
  - the `<title>`, if it carries a count;
  - `<meta name="description">`;
  - the hero `<p>` and `.dates` line;
  - the footer sentence.

  Agent B never touches the skeleton.

  **Nav pills for new units are yours (agent A).** For each new unit, add a link to the skeleton's `nav.top`, copying the markup of the last existing unit pill exactly (classes, `span.lw`/`.nf`/`.ns` spans), after it. `render_tpl.py` has already inserted the section placeholder `<!-- @@SECTION:<prefix>-N @@ -->`. If it is missing, report it to the orchestrator rather than adding it twice. The reviewer re-measures the phone nav afterwards.
- Never regenerate a merged section from only the new units' manifests; old units have none. Merge by editing the exploded part in place. `build_index.py` is the one exception, because it scans the cards themselves.

## Checks before reporting
- `assemble.py` clean for your parts (no duplicate ids, no dangling anchors into your parts, no full Name).
- Every `href="#…"` you wrote resolves: `grep -o 'href="#[^"]*"' <part> | sort -u`, checked against the ids in `out/<page>`.
- Counts agree: the `ix-counts` total equals `grep -c '<article class="card tech"' <workspace>/out/<page>`; the per-unit "K כרטיסים" in `series` equals the unit's card count; the unit count words match `project.json`.
- In existing mode, `diff` the exploded part against your version and confirm only additions and count changes.

## Report back (10 lines or fewer)
Each part's size, the number of cards or rows, the counts you updated and where, the assemble result, and any alias merge or principle merge you were unsure about.
